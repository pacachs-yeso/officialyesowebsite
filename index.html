Para sa Kabataan at Kalikasan.

Empowering youth for sustainable conservation

The Youth for Environment in Schools Organization (YES-O) is the premier school-based co-curricular organization aimed at fostering consciousness and deliberate action to protect, preserve, and restore our natural ecosystems.

Mission

To implement concrete, active eco-programs across school levels.

Vision

A community of youth champions with ingrained ecological integrity.

Objectives

Instill proper environmental stewardship and sustainable lifestyle habits.
