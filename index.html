About YES-O

What is YES-O?

Learn about the organization, its environmental role, and the programs supported by Pacac High School YES-O.

Youth for Environment in Schools Organization

Youth for Environment in Schools Organization, is a school-based organization recognized by the Department of Education that promotes environmental awareness, responsibility, and active participation among learners. Guided by its Constitution and By-Laws, YES-O organizes programs and activities focused on environmental protection, proper waste management, conservation of natural resources, cleanliness, and sustainability. It also develops learners’ leadership, teamwork, discipline, and sense of responsibility by involving them in projects within the school and community. Through these efforts, YES-O encourages learners to become responsible environmental stewards who take action for the protection of the environment and the welfare of their community.
