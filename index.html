STAGE 1: ABOUT YES-O

YOUTH FOR ENVIRONMENT IN SCHOOLS ORGANIZATION

MISSION & VISION

The Youth for Environment in Schools Organization (YES-O) is a DepEd-recognized student organization advocating environmental awareness, responsibility, and action.

Guided by clean energy and green principles, YES-O organizes waste management drives, tree planting activities, and ecological awareness campaigns to mold environmental stewards in Pacac High School!
